Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026.

gogCLI is a personal app used only by its owner, on the owner's own devices.

  • Google data accessed through this app is used only to help the owner with their own email, calendar, files and tasks.
  • No data is sold, shared with other people or used for advertising.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own computer. No server run by this app stores Google data.
  • Use of Google data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
